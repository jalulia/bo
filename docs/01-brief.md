# 01 · Brief

## The point

A soothing, funny, specific little world where Julia's cats live on her computer and act like *her* cats. Not a generic pet. Every behavior should make her say "that's so Bo."

Her words, collected: "so cute and soothing"; "cats are superlanguine"; "intention in the physical geometry of the walk"; "snappy-stretchy"; "the comraderie comfort part"; "RCT-quality scenery" (RollerCoaster Tycoon era pixel art).

## The cats

**Bo** (`coat: bo`, personality `bo`)
- Seal point & white Cornish Rex, 19 years old. Small, long-legged, tucked waist, huge ears, Roman nose, marcel-wave coat.
- Eyes: bright, light, shiny teal-blue (palette `boblue`).
- Temperament: aloof, sleepy, indifferent baseline. Watches the laser but rarely bothers ("so typical of Bo to watch the laser pointer but not usually give af"). Loves grass. Picky about being bathed (the watering can startles her).
- Favorite spots (from photos): the window sill pad in hard sun, the felt cube in the bookshelf, curled on blankets and pillows with one arm hanging out.

**Bunny** (`coat: bunny`, personality `bunny`)
- All white Cornish Rex, older and tinier than Bo. Gold/yellow eyes.
- Feisty: she wallops Bo and body-slams her; they chase and brawl.
- Mostly they nap and chill together. Wore a brown ribbon bow on her harness.

Their relationship is "love / hate / love": bond and tension drift; shared sunshine cools tension (the comfort zone); play-fights raise the bond.

## The toy box

Tabs: Comfort, Plants, Play, Treats, Garden (backyard only). One of each thing per place. Small things can sit on surfaces: the sill, bookcase top and shelves, the coffee table, and out back the potting shelves, the wire stand and the steps. Cats sometimes push them off.

## Out of scope (so far)

Sound design beyond the formant voice set; multiplayer; anything that would make it feel like a chore (needs are soft, nothing dies).
